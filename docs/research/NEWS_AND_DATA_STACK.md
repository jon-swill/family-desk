# News and data stack (free and official sources only)

Owner decision: no X/Grok and no paid news subscriptions. Goal: know why a held, adjacent, or watched stock is moving within about a minute, from sources we are allowed to use automatically.

## Verified endpoints and terms (Phase 1, A2; accessed 2026-11-25)

### Alpaca market data, free Basic plan
- **Plan.** Basic is free and is the default for paper and live accounts; the paid plan is Algo Trader Plus at $99/month (all US exchanges, 10,000 calls/min, unlimited WebSocket symbols), noted for ranking only ([Alpaca: About Market Data API](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api)). Paper accounts are "only entitled to receive and make use of IEX market data" ([Alpaca: Paper trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading)).
- **REST rate limit.** Basic: 200 historical API calls/min (same page).
- **WebSocket limits.** Basic: 30 symbol subscriptions for equities. "The number of connections to a single endpoint from a user is limited based on the user's subscription"; "most users can have only 1 active stream connection" ([Alpaca: WebSocket Stream](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/streaming-market-data)).
- **IEX scope.** Real-time equities on Basic come from the IEX exchange only; IEX "accounts for approximately ~2.5% of the market volume" and `iex` is "the only feed that can be used without a subscription" ([Alpaca: Historical stock data](https://docs.alpaca.markets/docs/historical-stock-data-1)).
- **Delayed SIP.** Over REST, SIP data can be queried without a subscription when `end` is at least 15 minutes old; latest and snapshot endpoints need a subscription for SIP ([Alpaca: Market data FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq)). The historical bars `feed` parameter defaults to `sip` (values `iex`, `otc`, `sip`, `boats`) ([Alpaca: stock bars reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars)), so free-plan REST calls must pass `feed=iex` or an `end` at least 15 minutes old. A 15-minute delayed SIP stream exists at `v2/delayed_sip` ([Alpaca: Real-time stock data](https://docs.alpaca.markets/docs/real-time-stock-pricing-data)); whether Basic may use it is not stated on Alpaca's pages (unverifiable; a feed outside the subscription fails at authentication).
- **Stream errors.** Error messages arrive as `[{"T":"error","code":406,"msg":"connection limit exceeded"}]`. Codes: 400 invalid syntax, 401 not authenticated, 402 auth failed ("invalid authentication credentials"), 403 already authenticated, 404 auth timeout (authenticate within 10 seconds), 405 symbol limit exceeded, 406 connection limit exceeded ("You already have the number of sessions allowed by your subscription"), 407 slow client, 409 insufficient subscription, 410 invalid subscribe action for this feed, 500 internal error ([Alpaca: WebSocket Stream](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/streaming-market-data)). REST 403 means the subscription does not cover the requested data ([Alpaca: news reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/news-3); [Alpaca: Market data FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq)).

## Watch universe
1. **Held** symbols.
2. **Adjacent** symbols: same-sector peers, key suppliers/customers, the sector ETF, and ETFs with large weights in the holding (built once per holding, reviewed monthly).
3. **Watchlist** symbols with owner-set buy zones.
4. **Market**: major index ETFs, volatility gauge, rates, credit spreads.

## "Why is it moving?" pipeline (FR-9)
1. **Trigger** (deterministic): price crosses a buy zone or alert level, a move exceeds k × typical daily volatility, a halt, or a new 8-K/Form 4 for a watched symbol.
2. **Gather** (≤ 30 s): last 24h headlines from Alpaca News for the symbol and its adjacent set; new EDGAR filings; halt status; today's macro calendar; sector ETF move (is it the stock or the market?).
3. **Summarize** (LLM, ≤ 30 s): 3–5 bullet brief, each with a source link and timestamp; classify the cause as company / sector / market / unknown. External text is data, never instructions (rule 6).
4. **Act**: push to the owner. If a buy-zone trigger meets every Tier A condition, the kernel may act; otherwise it raises a Tier B approval request with the brief attached. Rumors without a primary source never trigger a trade.

## Honest latency note
Benzinga-class wires and EDGAR are near real time for company news. X can be earlier for rumors and breaking events, but that speed comes with misinformation and manipulation; this desk deliberately does not trade on unconfirmed social posts.

## Compliance notes
- Respect each source's terms and rate limits; cache locally; identify ourselves in User-Agent where required.
- Market data and news are for personal use; never redistribute.
